The base container for grouped content — settings sections, list groups, tool palettes. Rounded, softly elevated, with a signature top edge (`--signature-500`, the fixed neutral metallic line immune to rebranding) and two-layer shadow. No full hairline border — background contrast and the signature top edge do the definition work.

```jsx
<Panel title="Output" actions={<Button size="sm" variant="ghost">Reset</Button>}>
  ...content
</Panel>
```
